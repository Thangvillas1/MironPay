How MironPay protects your wallet

MironPay is a custodial wallet: your keys are held by Circle, and nothing moves without your PIN. Here is exactly how that works — and what it means for you.

Who holds the keys

Each MironPay wallet is a Circle developer-controlled wallet. Circle generates and stores the private keys in its own infrastructure; MironPay's server asks Circle to sign a transaction only after it has checked your PIN.

That's why there is no seed phrase to lose — and also why you should treat your Google account and PIN as the keys to your money.

  1. 1You confirm a payment in the app and enter your PIN.
  2. 2The PIN travels with the request to MironPay's server, which checks it against a salted scrypt hash and its attempt limit.
  3. 3Only then does the server ask Circle to sign and send the transaction on Arc.

Layers of protection

Google sign-in

No MironPay password to leak. Your session is signed by Supabase Auth and checked on every request.

PIN checked on the server

Every send, swap, store payment, refund and dapp transaction is refused without the right PIN. Repeated wrong PINs lock the PIN for a while.

Two-factor sign-in (optional)

Turn on 2FA in Profile › Security: after your PIN, the app asks for a code from your authenticator app.

PIN reset by email

Changing or resetting your PIN needs a fresh code sent to your email, so a stolen unlocked phone alone can't change it.

PIN every time you open it

MironPay runs as an app installed on your Home Screen and asks for your PIN each time you open it. Log out removes your session and cached wallet data from the phone.

Lock wallet now

One tap in Profile › Security freezes the app behind your PIN. Login activity and trusted devices show where you're signed in.

Dapps can't surprise you

  • Only your main wallet, only on Arc Testnet, can be connected.
  • Each site is checked with WalletConnect Verify; known scam sites are blocked outright, unverified ones are labelled.
  • Requests are decoded into plain words. Unlimited token approvals and Permit signatures are marked as dangerous, and you can cap an approval before signing.
  • Transactions over 100 USDC need a second confirmation. Every dapp request is logged on our side.

Store payments

Each order has its own deposit address computed by the MironPayment Router contract, so a payment can only settle the order it was made for. Payers can verify the address on-chain before paying. Shops ask for the gold badge in the app, and MironPay reviews each request by hand.

What you should know

  • MironPay currently runs on Arc Testnet. Balances are test tokens with no real value.
  • Because keys are held by Circle on MironPay's behalf, MironPay is custodial: protect your Google account with its own 2-step verification.
  • Never share your PIN or an email code. MironPay will never ask for them outside the app.
