MironPayment Router

Store payments settle through one verified contract with no owner, no pause switch and no upgrades. Each order gets its own deposit address, and anyone can check an order's status on-chain.

How an order is paid

  1. 1 · ORDER

    The merchant's app builds an order: id, merchant, token (USDC), amount, expiry (15 minutes).

  2. 2 · DEPOSIT ADDRESS

    computeDepositAddress(order) gives a CREATE2 address unique to that order. The QR points there.

  3. 3 · PAY

    MironPay calls pay(order) with native USDC (refuses overpayment and expired orders). Any wallet can also send USDC to the address directly.

  4. 4 · SETTLE

    Anyone may call settle(order): it credits up to the order amount, sends 0.1% to the fee recipient and the rest to the merchant, and holds any excess.
