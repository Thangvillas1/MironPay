How to use MironPay

Everything in the app, step by step. MironPay runs on Arc Testnet: all amounts are test tokens.

Create your wallet

  1. On your phone, open mironpay.xyz/app. The page shows how to add MironPay to your Home Screen — iPhone: Share › Add to Home Screen in Safari or Chrome; Android: Install app in Chrome.
  2. Open MironPay from your Home Screen and tap Launch App. (MironPay only runs as an installed app, not in a browser tab.)
  3. Sign in with Google.
  4. Pick your @handle — it's how people find you to send money, and it can't be changed later.
  5. Create a 6-digit PIN and enter it again to confirm.
  6. MironPay sets up your wallet on Arc. Tap Enter MironPay.

There is no seed phrase to write down. Your sign-in plus your PIN protect the wallet.

PIN and unlocking

Each time you open the app, enter your PIN on Welcome back. Every send, swap, payment, refund and dapp request asks for it again.

  • Too many wrong PINs lock the PIN for a while; the screen shows how long.
  • Forgot it? Tap Forgot PIN?, enter the code we email you, and set a new one.
  • Change it any time in Profile › Security › Change PIN.

Send

  1. On Wallet, tap a token, then Send.
  2. Type a name, an @handle or a wallet address, or pick one of your contacts. Save people as contacts and mark favorites to find them faster.
  3. Type the amount on the keypad (or tap 10%, 25%, 50%, All), then Continue.
  4. Check the review — recipient, network, fee, total. Circle checks the address, your balance and the fee before you can send. Add a memo if you want, and tap Confirm & send.
  5. Enter your PIN. You'll see "Sending on Arc…", a tick, then the receipt.

Network fees on Arc are paid in USDC, so "All" keeps 0.01 USDC aside for the fee.

Receive

Tap the round button in the middle of the tab bar, then My code. Show the QR, or tap the address to copy it. To ask for an exact amount, pick the token and type the amount under the code — the QR updates to include it.

Swap

  1. Tap a token, then Swap. Pick what you pay (From) and what you get (To).
  2. Type the amount. You'll see the quote, the market rate beside it, the minimum you'll receive, and fees.
  3. Choose a slippage limit (Auto, 2% or 5%) and tap Swap now; the quote refreshes once more, then confirm with your PIN.

Scan and pay

Tap the round button: the camera opens full screen. Line the QR up in the square.

Activity

Every transaction, read from the chain and grouped by day. Filter by type — Transfers, Payments, Swaps, Dapps — and by date: Today, 7 days, 30 days, or pick days on the calendar. Tap a row for details, the transaction hash and a downloadable receipt.

Pay Mode, currency and token pages

  • Pay Mode (Profile): a payments-only home screen. It hides tokens, charts and swap; turn it off to get the full wallet back.
  • Conversion currency (Profile): show balances in USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, SGD or VND, at live exchange rates.
  • Token pages: tap a token on Wallet to see its price chart, your balance, that token's activity and a link to the Arc explorer.
  • Appearance (Profile): light or dark.

Set up your Store

Open the Store tab and tap Activate store mode. Give your shop a name and, if you like, a logo. Payments go to your MironPay wallet.

Want the gold badge? In store settings, choose Get the gold badge and pick a document type (ID card, passport or business licence). MironPay reviews the request by hand. Renaming the shop removes the badge until you verify again.

Products

In Store, open Products and tap Add: a name, a price in USDC and an optional product code. Tap a product to charge it straight from the keypad screen.

Take a payment

  1. In Store, tap Charge (or the + button), type the amount, add a note if you like, and tap Create payment code.
  2. Show the QR. It stays valid for 15 minutes; you can cancel the order.
  3. When the customer pays, the screen switches to Paid and the order turns Paid in your grid.
  4. Orders shows every order as a tile; search by order number, note or amount, and filter Open, Paid, Refunded or Closed. Tap an order for its details, the payer, the transaction hash and a downloadable receipt.

MironPay keeps 0.1% of each order as its fee.

Over- and underpayments

  • Paid too little: the order stays open and the QR now asks only for what's left. If the money came from another wallet, you can tap Collect now to accept what arrived.
  • Paid too much: the order is Paid for its amount; the extra is held. Tap Refund extra, choose the address that paid (or type one), and confirm with your PIN.

Revenue

Revenue shows what you received for Today, Yesterday, 7 days, 30 days or dates you pick, with a chart by day, month or year. It breaks out the number of orders, the MironPay fee (0.1%), network fees and any extra you refunded, next to your wallet balance.

Connect with WalletConnect

  1. On the dapp, choose Connect wallet › WalletConnect and scan its QR with MironPay.
  2. Check the site: Domain verified, not verified, does not match, or Known scam (blocked). Tap Connect.
  3. When the dapp asks you to sign or send, MironPay shows what the request does. Risky ones — unlimited token approvals, Permit signatures — are marked in red, and you can lower an approval to a set amount.
  4. Transactions over 100 USDC ask you to confirm twice.
  5. See and disconnect apps in Profile › Connected apps.

MironPay connects your main wallet on Arc Testnet only. A dapp that only supports Arc mainnet or other chains can't connect yet.

Points and leaderboard

Sends and swaps earn MironPay Points. Your tier — Newcomer, Builder, Trader, Elite — and progress to the next one are on Profile. Leaderboard shows the top 10 and your own rank.

Security settings

Profile › Security:

  • Change PIN — needs your current PIN and a code sent to your email.
  • Two-factor authentication — add an authenticator app; after your PIN, sign-in asks for its code.
  • Lock wallet now — freezes Send and Swap until you unlock with your PIN.
  • Trusted devices and Login activity — the device and session you’re signed in on now. A full history across devices isn't tracked yet.

Payment links (developers)

MironPay QR codes are standard EIP-681 payment requests pinned to Arc Testnet (chain ID 5042002), so any wallet can read them. USDC is Arc's native currency, so a USDC request carries a native value with 18 decimals; EURC and cirBTC use the token's transfer form with the token's own decimals.

10 USDC   ethereum:0xRECIPIENT@5042002?value=10000000000000000000
10 EURC   ethereum:0x89b50855aa3be2f677cd6303cec089b5f319d72a@5042002/transfer?address=0xRECIPIENT&uint256=10000000
Address   ethereum:0xRECIPIENT@5042002
