How it works

Sending money should take less time than reading this.

01 — AMOUNT

Type it on a real keypad.

Pick USDC, EURC or cirBTC, tap the amount, or jump to 10%, 25%, 50% or all of it. Your balance is right there.

02 — CONFIRM

One PIN. Every time.

A sheet slides up over your wallet. Six digits, checked on our server — nothing moves without them.

03 — DONE

Sent. On-chain.

Arc confirms in under a second, so the tick usually appears a moment later. You get a receipt with the transaction hash.

04 — HISTORY

Every payment, in one list.

Read straight from the chain and grouped by day. Filter transfers, payments, swaps and dapps, or jump to any date.
