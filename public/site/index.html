WHAT YOU CAN DO

One wallet for paying, getting paid and using dapps.

Send to anyone

Type an @name, a wallet address, or scan a payment QR. Confirm with your PIN; it lands on Arc in seconds.

Swap stablecoins

Move between USDC, EURC and cirBTC with a live quote, the market rate beside it, and your slippage limit.

Take payments

Turn on Store, type an amount, show the QR. Customers pay from MironPay or any wallet; you see it the moment it arrives.

Connect to dapps

Scan a WalletConnect code to use Arc apps. Every request shows what it does, flags risky approvals, and needs your PIN.

See every transaction

Activity reads straight from the chain, grouped by day, filterable by transfers, payments, swaps and dapps.

Earn points

Every dollar you pay with MironPay earns a point. Climb from Newcomer to Elite on the leaderboard.

FOR MERCHANTS

Accept USDC at the counter in three steps.

Payments go through MironPay's payment contract on Arc: each order gets its own deposit address, so a payment can't be mixed up with another. Fee: 0.1% per order.

  1. 1

    Charge

    Type the amount on the keypad, add a note if you like. The QR appears instantly.

  2. 2

    Customer scans

    They pay from MironPay or any Arc wallet. Paid too little? The QR asks only for what's left.

  3. 3

    Paid

    The order turns Paid on your grid. Paid too much? Refund the extra in one tap. Track it all in Revenue.

BUILT ON

Arc, USDC and Circle Wallets.

Arc is Circle's blockchain for stablecoin finance: fees are paid in USDC and transactions confirm in under a second. Your wallet is a Circle developer-controlled wallet; MironPay never shows you a seed phrase to lose.

How MironPay keeps your wallet safe → Contract addresses →
  • PIN checked on our server
    Every send, swap, payment and dapp request; wrong guesses lock the PIN for a while.
  • Optional 2-factor sign-in
    A code from your authenticator app on top of Google sign-in.
  • Readable dapp requests
    Unlimited approvals and Permit signatures are flagged; known scam sites are blocked.
