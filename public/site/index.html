DAPPS & SECURITY

Know who’s asking
before you sign.

Scan a WalletConnect code and MironPay shows who is requesting access and exactly what they want.

  • Sites rated verified, unverified or known scam — scams are blocked
  • Unlimited approvals and Permit signatures are flagged
  • Large transactions ask you to confirm twice
  • Every request needs your PIN

GET STARTED

Add it to your Home Screen. No app store.

MironPay runs only as an installed app. Open mironpay.xyz/app on your phone and it shows you how to install it — then open MironPay from your Home Screen.

IPHONE Safari or Chrome Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.
ANDROID Chrome Tap Install MironPay on the page, or open the ⋮ menu and choose Install app.
THEN Sign in and set a PIN Sign in with Google, pick your @handle and create a 6-digit PIN. Your wallet on Arc is ready.
